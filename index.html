What is Weather?

Short-Term Atmospheric Conditions

Weather refers to the day-to-day, short-term state of the atmosphere at a specific location and time. It fluctuates rapidly within minutes, hours, or days and includes elements like temperature, precipitation, humidity, wind speed, cloud cover, and atmospheric pressure.

मौसम (Weather) किसी स्थान विशेष पर वायुमंडल की दिन-प्रतिदिन और अल्पकालिक स्थिति को दर्शाता है। यह मिनटों, घंटों या दिनों में तेजी से बदलता है। इसमें तापमान, वर्षा, आर्द्रता, हवा की गति, बादलों का आवरण और वायुमंडलीय दबाव शामिल हैं।

Real-World Examples of Weather:

  • A clear, hot sunny morning in New Delhi turning into a rainy evening.
  • A sudden heavy monsoon thunderstorm for 2 hours in Mumbai.
  • Dense winter fog reducing visibility to zero on a December morning.

What is Climate?

Long-Term Weather Patterns (30+ Years)

Climate represents the long-term pattern, statistical average weather conditions, and seasonal variations of a region recorded over an extended period (typically 30 years or more). While weather changes daily, climate changes very slowly over decades.

जलवायु (Climate) किसी विशिष्ट क्षेत्र में लंबे समय (आमतौर पर 30 वर्ष या उससे अधिक) में दर्ज किए गए मौसम के औसत पैटर्न और मौसमी बदलावों को दर्शाती है। मौसम जहाँ रोज बदलता है, वहीं जलवायु दशकों में बहुत धीरे-धीरे बदलती है।

Real-World Examples of Climate:

  • The Hot Arid Desert climate of Thar in Rajasthan with low annual rainfall.
  • The Alpine Cold Desert climate of Leh & Ladakh in the high Himalayas.
  • The Tropical Monsoon climate along the humid coastal regions of Western Ghats.
Key Differences: Weather vs. Climate
Feature Weather (मौसम) Climate (जलवायु)
Time Scale Short-term (Hours to Days) Long-term (30+ Years average)
Variability Changes constantly within minutes or hours Changes very slowly over centuries or decades
Prediction Forecasted using radar & hourly satellites Analyzed using historical multi-decade statistics
